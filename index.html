Questions & Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about our Award Maker & Educational Tools.

Learn how teachers, homeschoolers, and parents utilize our free utilities to recognize student milestones and analyze literature.

How do I create and print a certificate using the Award Maker?

Enter the recipient's name, choose or type an award category, select a color theme (Royal Gold, Wattle Emerald, or Scholar Crimson), and click Print Certificate (A4) or Download PNG Image. No registration required.

Is the Cutech Certificate Generator 100% free?

Yes! It is completely free with zero hidden subscriptions, watermarks that don't intrude, and unlimited prints and downloads for teachers, parents, and schools.

Can I create custom certificates for subjects beyond reading?

Yes, select Custom Award Title in the award category dropdown to make certificates for STEM, writing, mathematics, sport, citizenship, or personal growth.

What curriculum does the Literary Technique Analyzer support?

The Literary Technique Analyzer is aligned with the Australian Curriculum for English (Years 6 to 12), helping students craft high-scoring TEEL/PEEL analytical paragraphs for NAPLAN, HSC, VCE, and QCE assessments.

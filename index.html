Our purpose

Why we exist

Literacy is more than a skill. It is a way into the world.

Cutech exists to make that way in clearer. We bring education and technology together to create learning experiences that are accessible, encouraging and built for real progress.

M

Our mission

Help more people become confident, capable readers and learners.

V

Our vision

A world where literacy creates possibility for everyone.
